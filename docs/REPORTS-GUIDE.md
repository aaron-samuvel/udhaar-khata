# Reading your ledger

The ledger is more useful with a daily rhythm:

## Daily collection check
At closing, compare payments received today against cash in hand. Any gap means an unlogged payment - log it now, not tomorrow.

## Top dues
Check the largest outstanding balances first. A customer with a large, old balance deserves a polite reminder before it goes stale (see [REMINDERS.md](REMINDERS.md)).

## Weekly review
- Are there reversal pairs you should learn from (recurring entry mistakes)?
- Any customer with zero activity for months? Consider a friendly check-in.
- Export a backup - weekly minimum, daily if the counter is busy.

## Reading amounts
All balances are exact - amounts are stored in integer paise, so what you see is what is owed, to the paisa.
