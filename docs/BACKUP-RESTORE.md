# Backup & Restore

Your ledger lives only in your browser, so backups are your responsibility.

## Taking a backup
1. Open the app and go to **Backup / Restore**.
2. Choose **Export backup** - a JSON backup file downloads.
3. Store it somewhere safe: Google Drive, email to yourself, or your computer.

## Restoring
1. Open the app on the target device.
2. Go to **Backup / Restore** -> **Import backup**.
3. Pick the backup file and confirm.

## Recommended schedule
- Daily for active shops (one tap, few seconds).
- Immediately after bulk data entry.

## Notes
- Backup files contain customer names and amounts - treat them as private.
- Keep at least two backup copies in different places.
