# Counter tips

Field-tested habits for accurate, fast ledger keeping:

- **Log at the moment**, not at closing: an entry takes seconds; reconstructing five evening transactions invites errors.
- **Say the amount back** while logging - the customer catches typos on the spot.
- **Quantities in notes, money in the amount field.** "2 kg atta, ₹96" -> note: "2 kg atta", amount: 96. The parser never reads units as rupees by design.
- **One customer, one khata**: avoid "misc" customers; they become untraceable dues.
- **Weekly top-dues review** beats daily anxiety: pick the 3 largest and remind gently (see [REMINDERS.md](REMINDERS.md)).
- **Backup at closing time** - make it part of the shutter-down ritual, like counting cash.
