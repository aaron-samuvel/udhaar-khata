# First-run onboarding (shop owner)

Day 1 with Udhaar Khata:

1. **Open** https://aaron-samuvel.github.io/udhaar-khata/ in Chrome on your shop phone.
2. **Install** it (Add to Home screen) so it works offline at the counter - [PWA-OFFLINE.md](PWA-OFFLINE.md).
3. **Add your customers** - name is enough; phone is optional (useful for reminders).
4. **Record udhaar as it happens** - one entry per transaction; notes can carry quantities ("2 kg atta"), amounts go in the money field only.
5. **Record payments the moment they come in** (jama).
6. **Take a backup** at closing time - one tap - [BACKUP-RESTORE.md](BACKUP-RESTORE.md).

## Rules of thumb
- Typed something wrong? Correct it with the fix/reversal action - history stays honest.
- Never share your backup file in group chats.
