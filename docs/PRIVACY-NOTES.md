# Privacy notes

## What the app collects
Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking, no account system.

## Where data lives
- Static deploy: your browser's `localStorage` on your device.
- Self-hosted: your own SQLite database file.

## What leaves your device
Nothing, by the app. (Your browser may make standard requests to GitHub Pages for assets.)

## Backups
Backup files contain your customers' names, phone numbers and dues. Store them privately; do not share them in group chats.

## For contributors
Never commit real shop data, customer names, or phone numbers - not in code, not in fixtures, not in issues.
