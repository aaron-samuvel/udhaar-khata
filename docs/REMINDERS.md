# Payment reminders (manual playbook)

The app shows who owes what; reaching out is up to you. Copy-paste friendly templates:

## WhatsApp / SMS - gentle
> Namaste {name} ji, aapke {shop} khata par {amount} baki hai (as on {date}). Jab aasuvidha ho, jama kar dijiye. Dhanyavaad!

## WhatsApp / SMS - festival-friendly
> {name} ji, tyohaar se pehle khata clear karna ho to aaj hi jama kar dijiye. Baki: {amount}. Shukriya!

## Tips
- Remind in the morning; evenings are busy at the counter.
- Never mention other customers' dues in a group message.
- Log the payment immediately after receiving it so the balance stays true.

## Privacy
Customer phone numbers stay in your device. Exported backups include them - share carefully.
